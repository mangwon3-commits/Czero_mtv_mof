### 1-7. ALF — aluminium formate, Al(HCOO)3  — carboxylate (metal formate) — ReO3-like cubic Al-formate net, Al(III), no OMS
- 한 줄: The cheapest and most deployable framework in the roster and the only one where CO2 is shown to DISPLACE preadsorbed water (70 % of dry dynamic capacity after RH 80.5 % presaturation), with a published DFT mechanism for why — but with almost no MTV headroom, which is the strategic disqualifier for this project's method.
